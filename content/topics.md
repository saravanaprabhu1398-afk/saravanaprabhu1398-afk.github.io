# Topic backlog

The weekly draft job takes the **first unchecked** item, drafts it into
`content/posts/` as `draft: true`, and ticks it here. Nothing publishes on its own.

Format: `- [ ] Title — angle, and the specific thing only you can say about it.`
The angle matters more than the title; it is what stops the draft being generic.
